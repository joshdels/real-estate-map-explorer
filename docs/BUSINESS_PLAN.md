# Business Plan

## Document status

- Planning date: November 2026
- Initial market: one Philippine province, beginning with one to three neighboring municipalities
- Initial milestone: 100 registered users by December 31, 2026
- Product stage: pre-commercial prototype using fictional, unverified sample data

This document describes the intended business. It does not imply that the current application verifies listings, brokers, titles, boundaries, valuations, or transactions.

## Business thesis

Real Estate Map Explorer should become a trusted, map-first marketplace for provincial properties that are difficult to discover or evaluate through conventional portals. It should prioritize affordable and underrepresented assets rather than trying to match national portals by raw listing volume.

The initial advantage is information quality:

- an understandable map location;
- property boundaries supplied by the advertiser and clearly identified as non-survey data;
- visible road access and nearby destinations;
- current price and availability;
- the advertiser's relationship to the property;
- dated verification signals; and
- a responsible person who can answer an inquiry.

The public promise should be:

> Find provincial property with clearer location, access, availability, and advertiser accountability.

## Priority inventory

Inventory should be accepted in this order:

1. Properties below the target municipality's median asking price.
2. Residential lots, modest houses, and long-term rentals.
3. Agricultural land and small farms.
4. Properties outside city centers with identifiable road access.
5. Bank-acquired or institutional assets when the advertiser is authorized.
6. Higher-value and developer inventory after affordable local supply is established.

The company must not claim that a low asking price represents fair market value. Asking prices, estimated dimensions, and supplied boundaries need separate labels.

## Customer groups

### Buyers and renters

They need current inventory, transparent prices, understandable locations, comparisons, and a safe way to contact an authorized advertiser. Search, comparison, saved properties, and inquiries should remain free.

### Property owners

They need help turning incomplete property information into a credible listing. Early revenue should come from assisted listing preparation, photography, mapping, and optional field visits—not from charging every owner before the marketplace has demand.

### Brokers and salespersons

They need credible inventory, a professional profile, qualified inquiries, and simple lead management. Professional credentials and authority to advertise must be checked independently and displayed precisely.

### Agencies, developers, banks, and cooperatives

They need bulk onboarding, portfolio pages, lead routing, reporting, and reliable inventory updates. This is a later B2B channel once the product demonstrates activity in at least one province.

### Local service businesses

Surveyors, photographers, movers, lenders, property managers, and other providers may eventually buy directory or sponsorship placement. Paid placement must be labeled and must not imply endorsement.

## Business model

The revenue sequence is:

1. Assisted listing and field services.
2. Broker and agency subscriptions after lead value is demonstrated.
3. Featured placement that never overrides relevance or hides its sponsored status.
4. Institutional onboarding and portfolio services.
5. Clearly labeled local-business sponsorships after meaningful consumer traffic exists.

The company should not take transaction commissions until it has obtained legal advice, defined its regulated role, and established the necessary licensed relationships. It should not hold reservation payments, deposits, or purchase funds during the initial stage.

## Marketplace launch model

This is a supply-first marketplace. The minimum useful launch in one area is:

- 30 current, sufficiently complete listings;
- at least three participating licensed brokers or authorized inventory partners;
- no listing without an accountable advertiser;
- availability reconfirmed at least every 30 days; and
- a manual response process for every buyer inquiry.

A new province should not launch merely because a province page can be created. It should meet the same minimum inventory and partner requirements.

## Competitive position

Established portals such as [Lamudi](https://www.lamudi.com.ph/) and [Dot Property](https://www.dotproperty.com.ph/) provide broad professional inventory. [OnePropertee](https://www.onepropertee.com/) provides a community-oriented discovery model, while [Carousell Property](https://www.carousell.ph/categories/property-102/) and Facebook groups provide substantial owner-direct and local reach. Newer services compete through professional verification, mapping, or lower subscription prices.

Real Estate Map Explorer should not copy their nationwide breadth. Its defensible position is:

- provincial depth rather than national thinness;
- affordable and overlooked inventory;
- map-first discovery;
- road and destination context;
- explicit verification facts and dates;
- disciplined removal of stale inventory; and
- assisted onboarding for owners who cannot produce a complete digital listing alone.

## Trust and verification model

The product must display verification as separate dated facts, not as one ambiguous badge:

1. **Identity checked** — the advertiser's identity was reviewed.
2. **Authority checked** — evidence of ownership or authority to advertise was reviewed.
3. **Professional credential checked** — applicable professional information was checked against the appropriate source.
4. **Location reviewed** — the submitted location is consistent with the advertised municipality.
5. **Availability confirmed** — the advertiser reconfirmed availability on the displayed date.
6. **Documents reviewed** — named documents were seen; this is not a guarantee of authenticity or clean title.
7. **Site visited** — a company representative visited the location on the displayed date.

The platform must never present map boundaries as surveys, asking prices as appraisals, document review as legal due diligence, or advertiser verification as a transaction guarantee.

## Operating principles

- Prefer 40 current listings over 400 copied or stale listings.
- Keep buyer access free during the market-creation stage.
- Never invent or scrape inventory without permission.
- Publish the full asking price when the advertiser supplies one.
- Protect exact locations when public disclosure creates privacy or safety risk.
- Record who changed a listing and when.
- Remove or suspend listings that cannot be reconfirmed.
- Separate paid ranking from organic relevance.
- Measure qualified buyer actions, not page views alone.

## North-star metric

The primary metric is:

> Current, sufficiently documented provincial listings receiving at least one qualified buyer action during the month.

A qualified action is a saved property with contact intent, a complete inquiry, a viewing request, or a confirmed advertiser conversation. Automated traffic, accidental clicks, and spam do not count.

## Principal risks

| Risk | Early control |
| --- | --- |
| Empty marketplace | Launch one municipality cluster and build supply first. |
| Fake or unauthorized listings | Require identity and authority evidence before publication. |
| Stale availability | Expire or suspend listings that are not reconfirmed. |
| Misleading boundaries | Label all non-survey outlines and preserve source information. |
| Privacy exposure | Publish approximate locations when exact disclosure is not authorized. |
| Unlicensed activity | Define the platform role and obtain Philippine legal advice before transaction involvement. |
| Poor mobile access | Maintain a mobile-first, low-friction experience. |
| Premature subscriptions | Charge recurring fees only after measurable lead value exists. |

## Expansion rule

Expand into the next municipality or province only when the current market has, for two consecutive months:

- at least 40 current listings;
- at least 10 qualified buyer actions;
- a median advertiser response time below 24 hours;
- at least 70% monthly availability reconfirmation; and
- one accountable local supply partner capable of ongoing onboarding.
