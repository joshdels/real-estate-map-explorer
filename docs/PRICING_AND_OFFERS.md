# Pricing and Offers

## Pricing principles

- Buyers and renters do not pay to search, compare, save, or inquire.
- Free supply plans create early marketplace liquidity.
- Early revenue comes from work performed, especially listing preparation and field services.
- Recurring subscriptions begin only after the product produces measurable advertiser value.
- Verification fees, service fees, subscriptions, and advertising charges must be described separately.
- No plan may imply that payment buys verification approval.

All amounts below are planning prices in Philippine pesos, inclusive of ordinary platform use but exclusive of separately quoted travel and third-party professional services. Final prices require tax, invoicing, and legal review.

## B2C offers

### Buyer or renter — Free

- Map and list search
- Filters and comparisons
- Saved properties and saved searches
- Availability alerts
- Advertiser inquiry
- Inaccurate-listing report

### Owner Basic — Free

- One active listing
- Up to five photos
- Approximate public map position
- Basic listing review
- 60-day publication
- Reconfirmation required every 30 days

The owner supplies accurate information and evidence of authority. Publication is subject to review and does not guarantee a buyer or tenant.

### Assisted Listing — PHP 999 one-time

- Staff-assisted data entry
- Photo and document checklist
- Map placement and owner-supplied boundary plotting
- Road-access and nearby-destination presentation
- Up to 15 photos
- 90-day publication
- One listing revision session

### Field Listing Service — PHP 2,500 to PHP 5,000

- Scheduled site visit within the supported service area
- Property photographs
- Short walkthrough video when practical
- GPS-assisted location and owner-guided boundary capture
- Road-access observations
- Seller or representative interview
- Assisted Listing inclusions

Travel outside the supported area and specialist services are quoted separately. Captured boundaries remain estimates unless prepared and certified by an appropriately qualified professional.

## B2B offers

### Founding Professional — Free during the first-100-user program

- Up to five active listings
- Public professional profile
- Lead notifications
- Founding-member label
- Monthly availability reconfirmation

Credential review and authority to advertise remain required. The founding label records early participation; it is not a quality guarantee.

### Broker Pro — PHP 999 per month or PHP 9,990 per year

- Up to 25 active listings
- Lead inbox and lead-status tracking
- Listing engagement analytics
- Three sponsored-placement credits
- Import and onboarding assistance
- Professional credential status

Do not begin charging until the platform can show relevant inquiries, time savings, or useful listing analytics in the broker's market.

### Agency — PHP 3,499 per month or PHP 34,990 per year

- Up to five team seats
- Up to 100 active listings
- Branded agency page
- Shared inquiry inbox
- Team and inventory reporting
- Ten sponsored-placement credits
- Priority onboarding support

### Institutional Portfolio — from PHP 7,500 per month

- Bulk inventory onboarding
- Portfolio or project pages
- Custom lead routing
- Inventory-update workflow
- Assigned account support
- Monthly performance report

Applicable to developers, banks, cooperatives, asset managers, and similar organizations. Custom data integration is separately scoped.

### Local service directory — later stage

- Basic verified business profile: free during initial directory building
- Enhanced profile: proposed PHP 199 per month
- Sponsored category placement: proposed PHP 499 per month

Sponsored results must be clearly labeled and must not be presented as professional endorsements.

## Launch promotions

### First 100 users

- Buyers receive free early access and availability alerts.
- Owners receive one free Basic listing.
- Founding professionals receive the professional plan free through February 2027.
- The first five local partners receive assisted onboarding.

### Referral rules

- An owner receives one Assisted Listing upgrade after a referred owner publishes an accepted listing.
- A professional receives one sponsored-placement credit after a referred professional completes credential review and publishes an accepted listing.
- Buyers receive earlier alerts or saved-search features, not cash for unqualified sign-ups.
- Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and copied listings do not qualify.

## Market reference points

Publicly advertised prices observed in November 2026 include:

- [MyTahanan](https://mytahanan.com/pricing): Pro at PHP 1,290 per month and Brokerage at PHP 4,490 per month.
- [Casa PH](https://casasearch.ph/pricing): professional plan at PHP 999 per month for up to 50 active listings.
- [PropDesk](https://propdesk.ph/pricing): Pro at PHP 499 per month and Business at PHP 999 per month.
- [PH Unlocked](https://www.phunlocked.com/pricing): agent plans beginning at PHP 2,500 per month.

These are reference points, not proof of demand or value. Prices and features can change and must be rechecked before a commercial launch.

## Pricing validation

Before making a subscription generally available, interview at least 10 professionals and test three questions:

1. Did the product generate a qualified inquiry or save meaningful operating time?
2. Which feature created that value?
3. Would the customer pay the proposed price for that result next month?

Keep service pricing if it sells before subscriptions do. Do not discount a weak recurring product into apparent demand.
